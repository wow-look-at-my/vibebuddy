## What's new

- The box works without the cable. Plug it in once and it pairs on its own. Then put it on Wi-Fi in Settings → Device, and VibeBuddy reaches it over your local network. The cable still wins when it is plugged in, and moving between them does not drop the link.
- A box links to one computer at a time. Another computer can take it over from Settings → Device.
- First-run setup now puts the box on Wi-Fi, and the Linux app gets a first-run guide too.
- Also: screenshots work over the network, the Linux Device tab scrolls, and joining Wi-Fi says whether it worked.
- Needs box firmware 0.5.0.
